## A hybrid architecture

**Stable business/product/function teams + selective capability centers or professional communities + dynamic outcome teams.** Business, work/technical and organizational/institutional compilation connect these elements.

Stable units retain customer relationships, product architecture, continuing operations and deep business context. Scarce, standardizable capabilities reusable across businesses may sit in a capability center or community. This does not require moving everyone out of existing departments; centers can evolve from them or span them.

## What capability centers supply

| Output | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| People | Skills, development, succession, careers, workload and professional depth |
| Capability assets | Standards, knowledge, cases, Skills, agents, tools and reusable methods |

Line Managers supply both people and assets. Value depends on effective use by outcome teams, not upload counts or the number of agents created.

## What constitutes a dynamic outcome team

A defined business outcome, appointed Loop Owner, callable capabilities with visible capacity, independent judgment gates, escalation and exit conditions make a team runnable. A temporary group chat does not establish these arrangements.

Formal membership and outcome responsibility create two management lines. Sponsors arbitrate capacity conflicts; significant cross-Sponsor conflicts go through existing operating governance. See [responsibility and authority](governance.md).

## Three kinds of compilation may constrain scale

![Original figure 5: three kinds of compilation](../assets/paper/three-compilations.jpeg)

Business compilation specifies outcomes, triggers and acceptance. Work/technical compilation makes tasks, data and execution runnable. Organizational/institutional compilation addresses roles, capacity, talent, incentives and employee rights. These iterate during operation. **The narrowest layer may determine expansion speed**: a testable management hypothesis, not a linear approval chain.

## 20 / 10 / 70: a future work-capacity hypothesis

![Original figure 6: work-capacity hypothesis](../assets/paper/capacity-hypothesis.jpeg)

| Proposed capacity | Work |
| --- | --- |
| Top 20%: human judgment | Direction, value choices, customer trust, complex judgment, commitments and final acceptance |
| Middle 10%: compilation | Business, technical and institutional compilation/governance turning intent, context, rules and authority into runnable work |
| Bottom 70%: agent execution | Retrieval, analysis, generation, testing, monitoring, coordination and other auditable execution |

**The denominator is effective work capacity across a target portfolio of Loops. It is not headcount, an FTE conversion, a staffing plan or a layoff target.** One person can supply both judgment and compilation. The actual mix must be tested by task, risk and maturity.

The middle layer may constrain the first Loop; reuse of validated knowledge, methods and agents may constrain expansion. Exploration starts with a few authorized teams and rules; expansion increases reuse and coordination; maturity may enable governed creation, operation, review and retirement of Loops.
