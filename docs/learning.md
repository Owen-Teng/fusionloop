# Learning: one cycle, three tracks, five stages; P2C2L and VKM

Organizational capability depends on trusted, authorized, callable and maintainable knowledge returning to real work, not document volume.

![Original figure 11: one cycle, three tracks, five stages](../assets/paper/learning-maturity.png)

## One cycle: return to the next real outcome

**Operating evidence → trigger → action → human judgment → acceptance → review and repair → review/testing → version update → next cycle.**

Record successes and failures. A retrospective alone does not establish learning; the reviewed change must face the next real cycle.

## Three tracks: outcome, responsibility and execution

| Track | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Business outcome | What was delivered, and did the customer or internal recipient accept it? |
| Organizational responsibility | Who owned, judged, approved and handled exceptions? |
| Capability execution | How did people, knowledge, Skills, agents and systems perform the work? |

Logs without outcomes cannot establish value. Outcomes without operating traces cannot distinguish clearer accountability, work redesign and agent effects.

## Five stages: M4 qualifies relationships, not single-Loop automation

| Stage | Required change |
| --- | --- |
| M0 · Baseline | Record cycle time, quality, cost, risk and human effort |
| M1 · Governed knowledge | Sources, owner, permissions, version and expiry |
| M2 · Controlled agents | Bounded agent execution, human judgment and takeover |
| M3 · FusionLoop | A complete single-Loop outcome with human–agent accountability |
| M4 · Workflow relationship | At least two M3 Loops connected through governed interfaces, producing an accepted overall result across Owners, teams or a second setting |

**M3 is the highest single-Loop stage. M4 qualifies relationships between mature Loops; it is not a higher automation percentage.**

## P2C2L: personal knowledge → organizational context → business Loop

This proposed method awaits team-operation validation. It has five gates:

1. Personal selection and authorization for defined purposes.
2. Governance of provenance, permissions, version and expiry.
3. A specified applicable work outcome.
4. Independent use and acceptance by someone other than the author.
5. Formal knowledge, rules or Skills after sustained reuse.

Uploading is an input. Independent results by a second person provide initial evidence of organizational capability; continuing reuse also requires observing review, rework and maintenance costs.

## VKM: AI-assisted knowledge management

Vibe Knowledge Management is the author's concept, drawing on the interaction pattern of Vibe Coding, not an established academic or industry standard. People express experience, conditions and boundaries. AI assists capture, organization, classification, linking, deduplication, formatting, reminders, retrieval and citation. The organization reviews, authorizes and publishes.

**VKM lowers participation cost; P2C2L governs transfer; FusionLoop tests value in real work.** Authorized people retain decisions about truth, sensitivity, permissions and formal release.

Employees retain notice, access, correction and appeal. Personal knowledge follows minimum necessity, explicit authorization and purpose limits. Operating records must not become automatic personnel judgments or unbounded monitoring.
