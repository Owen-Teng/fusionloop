## When agents execute work, what should change in the organization?

If information, knowledge and execution can coordinate directly around a shared outcome, what responsibilities should people retain? How should roles preserve professional homes? What should an organization reward?

**FusionLoop is a research collection on AI agents, human–AI collaboration and organizational design.** It includes a 37-page Chinese paper, bilingual guides and practical canvases for managers, HR practitioners and researchers exploring work design, roles and incentives. It connects human judgment, agent execution, organizational context and controlled learning around an accepted business outcome. Operating evidence then informs changes to organization, roles and incentives.

| Organization 2.0 | Roles 2.0 | Performance & incentives 2.0 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Stable business units, selective capability centers and dynamic outcome teams. | Clear formal authority and professional homes, with tasks and capabilities assembled around outcomes. | Direct results, verified reuse, talent development and succession, and agent/capability assets. |

## The research architecture

![Original figure 4: stable units, three kinds of compilation, dynamic outcome teams](assets/paper/organization-design.jpeg)

<sub>Original Chinese figure 4, p. 14. Capability centers are selective. The Loop Owner integrates work within delegated authority; professional sign-off, vetoes and formal personnel responsibilities remain distinct.</sub>

Business intent must become an outcome definition; technical design must make the work runnable; HR must translate work changes into institutions. The paper calls these **three kinds of compilation**.

## From an accepted outcome to the next cycle of capability

**FusionLoop is the work unit that carries this institutional hypothesis.** A Loop Owner integrates capabilities within Sponsor authorization and allocated capacity. Agents perform governable execution; authorized people retain consequential judgment, commitments and acceptance. Successes and failures enter the next cycle through review, testing and version control.

## Status and open questions

The source is an **August 2026 external-review revision**. FusionLoop's organizational effects, cross-person P2C2L reuse and FridayOS 2.0 team operation remain unvalidated. First-party personal use of FridayOS 1.0 is not evidence of organizational impact.

If net results fail to improve after review, rework and maintenance costs—or quality, fairness, succession or coordination burdens deteriorate across cycles—the design should be rolled back or the hypothesis revised.
