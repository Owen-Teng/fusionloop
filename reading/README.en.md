# FusionLoop Research Companion

![Research companion](../assets/reading-cover.svg)

**Read the evidence, examine the institutional design, and challenge the hypothesis.**

[**Download the complete reading package →**](https://github.com/Owen-Teng/fusionloop/releases/download/v0.3-reading/FusionLoop-Reading-Companion-v0.3.zip) · [中文](README.md) · [Research home](../README.en.md)

The package contains the author's unchanged 37-page Chinese paper, a Chinese reading-guide PDF, three Chinese thematic briefs, a curated catalog of 25 public sources, five bilingual template pairs, and an unchanged 51-page METR paper distributed under CC BY 4.0.

The other **24 external sources are official links, not bundled full texts**. After extraction, open `START-HERE.html`. The included PDFs, briefs and templates work offline; external source links require internet access.

[Full catalog](catalog.md) · [Machine-readable catalog](catalog.json) · [Attribution and licenses](ATTRIBUTION.md) · [Release](https://github.com/Owen-Teng/fusionloop/releases/tag/v0.3-reading)

The briefs are AI-assisted editorial reading aids, not translations of the external reports or new empirical studies. The original Chinese research remains authoritative. This curated companion is not the full underlying research corpus. It adds no claims of organizational validation or endorsement. The paper's evidence cutoff remains **21 August 2026**; this package was prepared on **23 October 2026**.
