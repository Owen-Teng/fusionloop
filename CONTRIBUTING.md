# Help improve FusionLoop

[首页](README.zh-CN.md) · [Home](README.md)

Useful contributions explain what happened, what was confusing, or what should change. You do not need code to contribute.

## Open an issue

- **Question:** the passage, your interpretation, and what remains unclear.
- **Counterexample:** the setting in which the proposal adds little value or makes work worse.
- **Practice report:** scope, method version, what you actually tried, acceptance, failures, effort, and remaining uncertainty. The [cycle record](templates/cycle-record.md) can help.
- **Correction:** a specific claim, a primary source, and the narrower wording it supports.

Include only material you are authorized to publish. Do not post private employee, customer, financial, or credential data. A constructed example must be labeled as such.

For text changes, explain the problem and proposed wording. Discuss substantial contributed material and reuse terms with the maintainer before submitting it. Contribution does not imply endorsement of the full proposal.

## 中文参与方式

欢迎提交问题、反例、真实尝试和修正建议，无需贡献代码。

- 提问时指出具体段落，以及哪里难以理解。
- 反例说明在什么场景下，方法没有价值或增加了负担。
- 实践报告保留实际范围、版本、验收、失败、人工投入和未知项，可参考[周期记录](templates/cycle-record.zh-CN.md)。
- 更正事实时，给出原始来源与它实际支持的说法。

只提交有权公开的材料。教学示例明确标注；不公开员工、客户、经营敏感信息或凭据。较大篇幅的贡献先与维护者明确范围和再使用条件。
