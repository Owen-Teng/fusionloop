# FusionLoop work canvas

[简体中文](loop-canvas.zh-CN.md) · [Worked example](../examples/research-brief.md) · [Home](../README.md)

Choose bounded work. Define the outcome and acceptance before assigning actions. Leave unknowns explicitly unresolved; an agent cannot make commitments for an actual owner.

## Working agreement

| Item | Fill in |
| --- | --- |
| Work name and version | To be completed |
| Current state | Draft / agreed, not started / running / accepted / returned / stopped |
| Sponsor and mandate | Who needs the outcome? What resources and actions are authorized? |
| Loop Owner | Who integrates the delivery? Where does their authority end? |
| Recipient and independent acceptance | Who can accept or return it? How are they separate from execution? |
| Participant capacity | Whose participation is needed, and has capacity been agreed? |

## Ten operating fields

| Field | Question | Agreement |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Trigger | What starts the work? What if inputs are incomplete? | To be completed |
| 2. Accepted outcome | Who receives what? Where does adjacent work begin? | To be completed |
| 3. One owner | Who integrates the outcome and escalates beyond their mandate? | To be completed |
| 4. Context and scope | Which sources, dates, and subjects are allowed? What is excluded? | To be completed |
| 5. Human responsibilities | Who sets goals, resolves conflicts, and makes professional judgments? | To be completed |
| 6. Agent and system responsibilities | Which reads, analyses, drafts, or actions are permitted? | To be completed |
| 7. Human decision points | What conditions require stopping for a named person's decision? | To be completed |
| 8. Exceptions and recovery | How are missing inputs, errors, and time limits handled? | To be completed |
| 9. Acceptance criteria | What will the recipient check, and what causes rejection? | To be completed |
| 10. Evidence and learning | What records remain? Who approves method changes and when are they reviewed? | To be completed |

## Before starting

- [ ] Outcome, owner, inputs, and recipient are agreed.
- [ ] Agent authority and human decision points are explicit.
- [ ] Important exceptions have a stop and recovery path.
- [ ] Cycle time, human effort, rework, and quality will be recorded.
- [ ] External publication and real-system changes have appropriate authorization.

This canvas describes a design. Use a [cycle record](cycle-record.md) for execution and acceptance. Completed checkboxes do not establish business effects.
